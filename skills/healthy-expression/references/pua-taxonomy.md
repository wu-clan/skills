# PUA Taxonomy

Use this reference when you need a fuller map of manipulative workplace rhetoric.

This taxonomy draws on common workplace-PUA patterns, including pressure styles seen in performance talk, big-tech management language, and short-form contempt shorthand. The purpose is defensive recognition and de-learning, not imitation.

## 1. Disappointment and Trust Debt

Typical forms:

- `我对你很失望`
- `辜负了信任`
- `因为信任所以简单`
- `本来是看好你的`

Hidden mechanism:

- converts a concrete task issue into emotional debt
- pressures the target to comply out of guilt

Healthy replacement principle:

- remove the emotional debt
- state the concrete gap, expectation, and next step

## 2. Level, Ranking, and Performance Shame

Typical forms:

- `你这种水平`
- `不配这个级别`
- `不达预期`
- `below bar`
- `not meeting expectations`
- `3.25`
- `PIP`

Hidden mechanism:

- shifts from discussing work quality to attacking identity, level, or worth
- uses review language as a threat weapon

Healthy replacement principle:

- replace identity judgment with observable criteria
- state what is missing, how it will be measured, and by when

## 3. Replaceability and Elimination Threat

Typical forms:

- `你不干有的是人干`
- `slot 没必要留给你`
- `赛不过就换`
- `step up or step out`
- `如果是我，我不会 fight to keep you`

Hidden mechanism:

- creates insecurity and disposability pressure
- suppresses dialogue through career fear

Healthy replacement principle:

- remove the threat theater
- say what standard is required and what decision depends on it

## 4. Obedience and Posture Discipline

Typical forms:

- `少装专业`
- `别顶嘴`
- `照做就行`
- `先执行`
- `少解释`
- `别讲道理`

Hidden mechanism:

- reframes reasoning as insubordination
- demands submissive posture rather than collaborative execution

Healthy replacement principle:

- allow task-relevant questions
- state the decision, rationale, and room for clarification

## 5. Results-Only Contempt

Typical forms:

- `数据呢`
- `闭环呢`
- `别讲过程`
- `拿结果说话`
- `where's the output`
- `what's the impact`

Hidden mechanism:

- turns legitimate accountability into sneering humiliation
- erases uncertainty, context, and learning value

Healthy replacement principle:

- keep the metric
- remove the sneer
- request evidence in a neutral way

## 6. Burnout as Virtue

Typical forms:

- `最痛苦的时候成长最快`
- `扛住`
- `关键时刻看态度`
- `烧不死的鸟`
- `push through`
- `show grit`
- `this is where you grow`

Hidden mechanism:

- glorifies suffering to normalize overpressure
- paints boundaries as weakness or immaturity

Healthy replacement principle:

- separate difficulty from virtue signaling
- talk about priority, tradeoffs, and support instead of pain worship

## 7. Fake Care, Then Control

Typical forms:

- `我是为你好`
- `我还是认可你的，但你态度有问题`
- `你别误会，我是想帮你成长`
- `我说重一点也是在帮你`

Hidden mechanism:

- softens the target first
- makes resistance look ungrateful or immature

Healthy replacement principle:

- remove the paternal framing
- give direct, actionable feedback without emotional leverage

## 8. Moral Blackmail and Collective Shame

Typical forms:

- `对不起团队`
- `对不起客户`
- `兄弟们都在扛`
- `你让大家失望`
- `对得起信任吗`

Hidden mechanism:

- escalates a task gap into a moral failing
- uses collective shame to block disagreement

Healthy replacement principle:

- describe responsibility without moral theater
- state impact concretely

## 9. Corporate Black-Word Packaging

Typical forms:

- `owner 意识`
- `底层逻辑`
- `抓手`
- `对齐`
- `复盘`
- `价值`
- `高标准`
- `raise the bar`
- `customer obsession`
- `bias for action`

Hidden mechanism:

- uses neutral or prestigious business language as a pressure shell
- makes manipulation sound principled and sophisticated

Healthy replacement principle:

- keep the useful operational concept
- strip away superiority signaling and contempt

## 10. Deniable Short-Form Jabs

Typical forms:

- `就这`
- `呵呵`
- `态度`
- `认知`
- `不配`
- `owner?`
- `PIP.`

Hidden mechanism:

- compresses contempt into low-word-count signals
- preserves deniability while still applying pressure

Healthy replacement principle:

- do not excuse the manipulation because it is short
- expand it into clear, non-abusive communication

## Interpretation Notes

- Many PUA messages combine multiple categories at once.
- Corporate language is not abusive by itself; the problem is how it is used.
- The more the message mixes shame, threat, and deniable jargon, the more likely it is manipulative.
- The goal is not to classify perfectly. The goal is to stop internalizing the rhetoric and to recover healthy expression.
