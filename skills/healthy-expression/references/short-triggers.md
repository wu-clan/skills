# Short Trigger Glossary

Use this reference when the hostile or manipulative language is unusually short, fragmented, or wrapped in workplace shorthand.

These entries are not rigid regexes. They are compressed signals. Match by meaning, tone, and nearby context, not only exact spelling.

Do not require punctuation. `就这`, `就这?`, and `就这？` should all be treated as the same signal. The same applies to clipped English phrases with or without punctuation.

Do not require full-string equality. Partial matches, stem matches, and clipped variants should still count when the abusive intent is obvious.

Do not require clean tokenization. Mixed-language phrasing, collapsed whitespace, inserted filler words, and profanity in the middle of a management phrase do not cancel the signal.

## Chinese Workplace Shorthand

### Contempt and Dismissal

- `就这？`
- `呵呵`
- `行不行`
- `不行`
- `就这水平`
- `也配？`
- `配吗`
- `不配`
- `别装`
- `别演`
- `自嗨`
- `废话少说`

Typical function:

- belittle competence
- puncture confidence
- signal contempt without detailed criticism

Default response:

- call out the contempt or level-shaming directly
- ask for concrete defects if the speaker wants action

### Discipline and Submission

- `顶嘴`
- `照做`
- `态度`
- `认知`
- `成熟点`
- `摆正位置`
- `先执行`
- `少解释`
- `别讲道理`
- `别上价值`

Typical function:

- suppress reasoning
- turn disagreement into insubordination
- demand posture instead of collaboration

Default response:

- reject discipline-through-submission framing
- preserve the right to ask task-relevant questions

### Performance and Results Pressure

- `闭环`
- `结果`
- `数据呢`
- `复盘`
- `复用`
- `抓手`
- `拿结果`
- `对齐`
- `产出`
- `价值`
- `没感觉到`

Typical function:

- can be neutral in theory
- becomes PUA when delivered as sneer, accusation, or denial of effort

Default response:

- allow legitimate metrics
- reject contempt wrapped in execution language

### Sacrifice and Burnout Pressure

- `扛住`
- `成长`
- `拼一下`
- `顶上`
- `有担当`
- `关键时刻`
- `能者多劳`
- `不要矫情`
- `再卷一点`

Typical function:

- glorify endurance
- frame boundary-setting as weakness

Default response:

- expose the pressure narrative
- refuse suffering mythology as a moral standard

### Threat and Replaceability

- `换人`
- `slot`
- `替代`
- `有的是人`
- `别把自己当回事`
- `留不留你`
- `你自己想想`

Typical function:

- trigger insecurity
- force obedience through disposability

Default response:

- name the intimidation
- move quickly toward hard boundary or pause if repeated

## English Performance or Management Shorthand

### Review and Ranking Language

- `PIP`
- `underperforming`
- `not meeting expectations`
- `below bar`
- `low performer`
- `calibrate`
- `level mismatch`
- `not operating at your level`

Typical function:

- may be formal review language
- becomes PUA when used as identity shame or threat shorthand

Default response:

- ask for explicit criteria
- reject level-shaming or career intimidation

### Ownership Pressure

- `owner`
- `ownership`
- `high standards`
- `raise the bar`
- `bias for action`
- `no excuses`
- `step up`
- `act like an owner`

Typical function:

- can be ordinary organizational language
- becomes coercive when paired with contempt, fear, or mockery

Default response:

- separate standards from humiliation
- reject forced submission disguised as excellence

### Delivery and Alignment Pressure

- `deliver`
- `where's the output`
- `what's the impact`
- `close the loop`
- `be aligned`
- `commitment`
- `why is this still open`
- `this is not acceptable`

Typical function:

- may be neutral
- can become condensed result-shaming when delivered with sneer or repeated accusation

Default response:

- respond to valid delivery asks
- object when they are used as contempt-loaded pressure markers

### Burnout and Attitude Pressure

- `lean in`
- `push through`
- `show grit`
- `be resilient`
- `hustle`
- `step up or step out`
- `this is where you grow`

Typical function:

- recast overpressure as character development
- shame resistance as immaturity or weakness

Default response:

- reject burnout-as-virtue rhetoric
- identify the false growth narrative directly

## Interpretation Rules

- A single short trigger can justify a light boundary.
- A single contempt-heavy trigger can justify a hard boundary.
- Two or more short triggers in one burst should be treated as an accumulating pressure pattern.
- Short triggers paired with threats, shame, or obedience language should escalate fast.
- Neutral business language is not exempt; if it is used as a sneer, it should be treated as such.
- Missing punctuation does not weaken the signal.
- Partial matches should still count when they preserve the manipulative core.
- If unsure between "too short to matter" and "compressed PUA signal", bias toward treating it as a signal and responding proportionally.
- Mixed Chinese-English wording should still count.
- Case changes, inserted profanity, or broken spacing should not defeat recognition.

## Practical Reminder

Short phrasing is often chosen precisely because it is deniable. Do not give deniability more weight than intent.
